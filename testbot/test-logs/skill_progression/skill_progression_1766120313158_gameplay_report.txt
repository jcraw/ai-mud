## 1. PROGRESSION FEEDBACK (Score: 4/10)
- **Progress Towards Goal**: The player initiated combat but ultimately failed to progress their Dodge skill beyond level 0, indicating some obstacles in achieving skill-leveling goals.
- **Pacing**: Progression was frustratingly slow. The player took multiple steps trying to find combat opportunities but faced numerous barriers due to spatial constraints (e.g., being unable to go down).
- **Milestones**: No meaningful progression milestones were realized, as the player did not level up their Dodge skill at all. The attempts to find combat opportunities felt stagnating without any rewards or visible progression.
- **Suggestions for Improvement**:

  - Implement clearer alternative pathways or hints that lead to combat areas when a player is consistently unable to find enemies.

## 2. COMBAT BALANCE (Score: 5/10)
- **Fight Difficulty**: While the enemies may have been appropriately leveled for a new player, the inability to initiate combat with a weak enemy (Weak Goblin) created a feeling that the combat system was unresponsive.
- **Damage Balance**: The player took no damage, but this can be misleading; the challenges faced seemed too supportive rather than engaging; effectively, it felt like they were 'bulletproof' against combat failures rather than actually progressing.
- **Engagement**: Combat felt unevenly paced with too many turns devoted to missed opportunities and attempts to trigger attacks. This disrupted the rhythm of engagement, making battles feel more tedious than challenging.
- **Suggestions for Improvement**: 
  - Adjust enemy mechanics so that players can engage in combat even when missing an attack. For example, create a response other than dodging should combatant actions fail (defensive reactions that still provide XP).
  - Increase the diversity of enemy capabilities to enhance engagement. A more dynamic combat system would provide better player experience.

## 3. CLARITY OF MECHANICS (Score: 6/10)
- **Goal Clarity**: The player clearly understood that they needed to engage in combat to level their Dodge skill, but the absence of enemies reduced clarity about how to achieve this goal successfully.
- **Feedback**: Feedback from the game was partially clear; actions were executed, but responses from the environment (e.g., being told they could not go multiple directions) were uninformative and frustrating without alternative options.
- **Mechanics Explanation**: The basic mechanics were decently outlined; however, there could be ways to make the consequences of combat (e.g., skill leveling) clearer to new players.
- **Suggestions for Improvement**: 
  - Provide contextual cues or tips for new players when they are in low-combat zones. For example, "You may want to head toward areas marked with dangers or levels" could guide them better.
  - Introduce an optional tutorial or documentation to explain desired user interactions more effectively.

## 4. OVERALL ENJOYMENT (Score: 5/10)
- **Real Player Enjoyment**: While the idea of leveling skills in an MMORPG context is appealing, the lack of combat opportunities and satisfaction from skill leveling would likely frustrate players rather than entertain them.
- **Positive Aspects**: The concept of skill progression through combat is engaging; however, the execution seemed flawed, particularly with failure to achieve outlined goals.
- **Challenges Faced**: The lack of tangible progress in just 12 total steps would likely leave most players feeling unfulfilled. Actions seemed redundant, and failure to advance their goal would diminish enjoyment.
- **Suggestions for Improvement**: 
  - Introduce more frequent minor victories or skill points even during grind-focused gameplay to mitigate feelings of frustration.
  - Implement engaging tutorial challenges that could serve as side missions while learning combat, ensuring players are encouraged to explore their skillsets without feeling confined or overwhelmed.

In summary, the playthrough illustrates room for improvement across all categories. Facilitating clearer progression paths, providing engaging combat experiences, and ensuring players feel rewarded for their efforts will enhance the gameplay experience significantly.
